CodeTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

CodeTrail is an educational puzzle game that teaches children the basics of programming. It is designed to be used by children, so this policy is deliberately simple: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Data we collect

None. CodeTrail has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software development kits. The app does not request network access and never sends anything off the device.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (player names typed into the game, stars earned, levels solved, chosen hero and settings such as language and sound) is saved only on the device, in the app's private storage. On the web version it is saved in the browser's local storage. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's site data, deletes it. Nothing is backed up or synced by CodeTrail itself; Android's own app backup may include it according to your device settings.

Children

CodeTrail is suitable for children of all ages. Because the app collects no data, no information about children is ever gathered or transmitted.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked through the project page: github.com/acidsnare/CodeTrail/issues.
